# Pricing — Fusion Calling

> Fusion Calling is a white-label AI voice automation platform: human-like AI phone agents handle inbound and outbound calls 24/7 (appointment scheduling, lead qualification, FAQ handling, voicemails, receptionist coverage), and agencies resell the platform under their own brand and domain. Month-to-month billing, no long-term contracts, 14-day money-back guarantee, 3-day free trial.

## Business plans (for businesses that want AI answering their calls)

### Starter
- Price: $149/month
- Limits: 500 included minutes/month, $0.30/min overage
- Features: 24/7 AI call answering, appointment booking, lead qualification, call transfers to humans, call recordings and transcripts, CRM and calendar integrations, SMS follow-ups

### Pro
- Price: $249/month
- Limits: 1,050 included minutes/month, $0.25/min overage
- Features: Everything in Starter with a larger included-minute allowance

### Enterprise
- Price: $497/month
- Limits: 2,100 included minutes/month, $0.20/min overage
- Features: Everything in Pro with the largest included-minute allowance

## Partner / white-label plans (for agencies and resellers)

### Starter Partner
- Price: $99/month
- Limits: 6 client sub-accounts
- Features: White-label dashboard, custom domain (e.g. app.youragency.com), your own branding and pricing, keep 100% of what you charge clients, 3-day free trial

### Growth Partner
- Price: $299/month
- Limits: 20 client sub-accounts
- Features: Same as Starter Partner with more sub-accounts

### Scale Partner
- Price: $499/month
- Limits: Unlimited sub-accounts
- Features: Same as Starter Partner with no sub-account cap

## Notes
- Partners keep 100% of client revenue; the partner plan is the wholesale cost. Guided white-label launch takes about 24 hours; partner applications are reviewed within 48 hours.
- Voice providers: Vapi, Retell AI, and ElevenLabs — existing agent configurations can be imported without rebuilding.
- Integrations: HubSpot, GoHighLevel, Google/Outlook calendars, Cal.com, 2,200+ apps via Zapier, Stripe rebilling.
